# 2026-07-28 — the day the model changed, and Day 1 got made

Long session. Two halves: a strategy rebuild in the morning, and Day 1 of Take·5 built,
recorded and finished in the afternoon.

## 1. The ad was stopped

Final days were producing leads **under $2** with **zero snacks** — before or after signing up.
Jonny's read: Meta had found people who sign up for anything. Correct, and the important
lesson is in [[02 Rules & Decisions]]: *a falling CPL is not automatically good news; the
optimizer always succeeds at the event you give it.*

Full test-1 readout in [[04 Waitlist & Validation]]. Headline: **acquisition solved
($2–10/lead), retention never demonstrated (one cross-day return in ~21 signups, and Meta had
to pay to bring him back).**

The Monday welcome email was cancelled — the list gets the course instead, once Day 1 has a page.

## 2. The model was rebuilt (the long argument)

It started with Jonny pushing back on "everything on the site stays free forever" — *"that's
not how Headspace works."* He was right, and checking it properly changed the architecture.

**Verified** (sources in [[05 Competitive Landscape]]): Headspace's 2013 free layer was Take10
and *nothing else* except support content — podcasts, beginner videos, a timer, a forum.
Take15/Take20/the Journey were paid from day one. Today, nothing at all is free in-app after
the trial.

**So the mapping was backwards.** Our snack library isn't "Take10's repeatable days" — it is
structurally the thing Headspace *charged for*. Take·5 is Take10. The library is the membership.

Also researched Calm, which turned out more relevant in places — the pre-launch "Do Nothing for
2 Minutes" toy, Day 1 of every program permanently free (this is where our ungated Day 1 came
from), and the $10→$40/yr price test with no drop-off.

**The funnel that fell out of it:** Day 1 ungated → the email is the price of Day 2 → a Lead
becomes structurally impossible without a completed lesson. The metric misalignment fixes
itself without touching the optimizer. Full model in [[01 Project Status]].

Jonny's other correction, taken: *stop treating the current ad's promise as a constraint on the
future business model — a new ad can be run.*

## 3. Take·5 Day 1 — built, recorded, done

**Script**: v1 3:38 → v4 **2:58**. Biggest change was structural — fingertips on the muscle
BEFORE the teach, so the idea lands on something they can already feel. Then two teach lines cut
at Jonny's call. Five windows in v2 turned out to be *shorter than the line takes to say*,
including both of Jonny's own rewrites; the fix was measuring estimated speaking time and
sizing every window to it.

**Booth built**: `../take5-recorder.html`. The feature that mattered was the **0.8s lead-in** —
showing each line slightly early so the natural read-then-speak lands on the beat.
Delivery lag across three takes: **+0.88s → +0.24s → +0.08s**.

**Three takes.** Take 3 is final. Vocal fry (Jonny's own catch) measured down from 28.9% to
20.3% of voiced frames after breathing before each line and sitting slightly higher in pitch.
Cleaned from a −38 dBFS floor to **−57.5, SNR 32.3 dB** — NYC street noise turned out to be
almost entirely sub-500 Hz, which is why the chain works so well.

**Bugs found and fixed in the master**: the ritual breath was being cross-dissolved into by the
face arriving mid-exhale (the "weird morph"); the head's breathing was nearly invisible (8%
swell with no vertical motion — now 18.5% plus a body lift and a contracting shadow); the
clench face was a literal flat `lineTo` and read as stern (now a narrowed smile); four captions
were killing their own last words.

**Ad cut**: recorded, cleaned, **27.4s**. Two seconds of dead air excised — 1.2s of it right
after the hook, where the calf reel's retention curve historically bled. New closing line
*"That's day one. There are four more."* and an end card that names the course and points at
`settlemoves.com/take5`.
