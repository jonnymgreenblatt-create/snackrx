Settings
Your moods, results and streaks stay in this browser. On the live site, Meta's ad pixel counts visits, basic activity and waitlist signups; signups are matched to ads using a privacy-protected, hashed version of your email. It never sees your moods, ratings or results. If you've joined the waitlist, we also count your return visits and completed snacks — that same hashed email and a session count, never what happened in them.
Settle is a wellness tool, not medical care. It doesn't diagnose or treat anxiety disorders. If anxiety is disrupting your life, a clinician can help. Stop any exercise that causes pain, chest discomfort, or dizziness.
