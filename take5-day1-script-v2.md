## Opening — *calm, unhurried, they just arrived*

**[0:00]** Day one. Unclench.

**[0:04]** Take five. Sit tall. Feel your feet on the floor.

**[0:11]** Let your hands rest. One slow breath.

**[0:15]** *(orb: "breathe in" — the full 4s inhale, no voice)*

**[0:19]** *(orb: "breathe out, slow" — the full 6s exhale, ends 0:25 before anything else moves)*

## Find it first

**[0:26]** Fingertips to the corners of your jaw, just in front of your ears.

**[0:33]** Now clench your teeth, gently. Feel that small bulge under your fingertips?

**[0:40]** That's the masseter.

## The teach — *straight off the masseter, hands still on it*

**[0:43]** This one muscle gets more of your brain's attention than your entire back does. So it's a shortcut to relaxation.

**[0:54]** And unclench. Feel it soften. Just let it go.

**[1:00]** Let's begin.

## The massage — *cues get sparser; the silence between them is part of the product*

**[1:03]** Small, slow circles. Both sides. No hurry.

**[1:14]** If you find a tender spot, stay with it. Gentle.

**[1:24]** Now circle the other way.

**[1:34]** If it feels nice, let your eyes close.

**[1:44]** *(whispered)* Just keep breathing. Slow and easy.

## The release

**[1:54]** Now let the hands rest. Teeth apart. Lips closed.

**[2:05]** Let the tongue rest low. The jaw just hangs.

**[2:15]** One more slow breath, out through the nose.

## Still moment + close — *the smile goes on here*

**[2:25]** *(whispered)* Now be still a moment. Just notice what's humming.

*(five seconds of nothing — hold it)*

**[2:36]** Notice how much you were holding.

**[2:41]** So from now on: at a red light, or a loading screen. Teeth apart, jaw relaxed. That's your cue now.

**[2:52]** That's day one. See you tomorrow.

*(ends 2:58)*
